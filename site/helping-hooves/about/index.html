Preserving heritage, building community, creating meaningful connections

Helping Hoof Initiative (hhi™) creates safe, inclusive spaces where horses and humans heal, learn and grow together. Through equine-assisted learning (EAL) programs and community partnerships, we empower children, youth, adults and seniors — supporting emotional, social, physical well-being.

We center our work in Prospect Park, a landscape intentionally designed for horses, and champion the preservation of the equestrian heritage that remains a living part of our city.

Explore our programs

Our mission in action

  1. Preserve

    Protect the physical infrastructure that allows horses to remain part of Prospect Park. Document, maintain, and advocate for the equestrian landscape and its historic infrastructure.

  2. Reactivate

    Bring historic equine spaces and heritage back into public life through programs, events and community access.

  3. Connect

    Create opportunities for today’s communities to experience and understand this living heritage — uniting horses, people and the city.

Our three pillars of impact

Brooklyn community

Helping Hoof Initiative (hhi™) creates community connections through equine heritage, youth mentoring, intergenerational programming, and accessible public events in Prospect Park.

Heritage preservation

Helping Hoof Initiative (hhi™) seeks to preserve the history of New York’s equestrian culture through partnerships such as Prospect Park Alliance (PPA) while bringing that history back into public life.

People + park + horse

Helping Hoof Initiative (hhi™) uses historic equine infrastructure as a platform for youth development, intergenerational connection, environmental stewardship, and meaningful community programming in Prospect Park.
