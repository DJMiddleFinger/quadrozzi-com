Our Mission

Horses & People,
Better Together

Helping Hooves is the Quadrozzi family's community work with horses. It is carried out by Helping Hoof, Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, alongside Be•Brooklyn Equine at Prospect Park Stable.

The mission has four parts:

01

Second-Career Horses

Giving horses whose first careers didn't work out a new start in "healthy, nurturing, loving environments."

02

Therapy & Learning

Promoting equine-assisted therapy and assisted learning.

03

Riding for Everyone

Making riding accessible to people "with physical, emotional, social, or economic challenges."

04

Safe Streets & Trails

Promoting public infrastructure that lets urban horses live and thrive, safely for horses and people alike.

Mission as published by Helping Hoof (6sqft, 2023).

In the Community

What We Do

From the riding ring to Red Hook's community gardens, the work reaches well beyond the barn.

A Fresh Start

Second-Career Horses

Many of the stable's horses didn't work out in their first jobs and were resold or rehomed before arriving in Brooklyn. With retraining, focused healthcare and plenty of time with other horses and the people who care for them, each one finds a role that suits its mind and body.

Some become loyal student horses, some trusty trail horses, and most enjoy both jobs. Xiana Quadrozzi, Program Director, Be•Brooklyn Equine
Therapeutic Riding
Partners in Therapy

Therapeutic Riding

In 2019, Prospect Park Stable began providing horses to GallopNYC, the New York therapeutic riding nonprofit, for sessions on Prospect Park's Bowling Green. The horses had a month of specialized training to prepare for the program, which serves children and adults with disabilities, including autism.

  • Therapeutic riding
  • Specially trained horses
  • Children & adults
  • Bowling Green, Prospect Park

Youth Programs
Young Riders

Youth Programs

In the summer immersion program, riders ages 6 to 15 spend the whole day at the stable. They get their horse ready, ride in the park, and then learn a different part of horse history, behavior and healthcare each week.

After-school and weekend programs teach both riding and complete horse care, from grooming to groundwork.

  • Summer immersion, ages 6–15
  • After-school & weekend programs
  • Horse care & safety
  • Lessons in Prospect Park

Since 2019

From Stable to Garden

The stable's manure is composted at the family's GBX terminal, under the mentorship of the nonprofit Red Hook Farms, and given to Red Hook community gardens. Bedding comes from wood shavings donated by local Brooklyn woodworkers.

About GBX →
June 2019

The Horse Parade Returns

The Prospect Park Equine Rally rode from the Horse Tamers statues along the Bridle Path to the riding circle known as "the Q." It was Brooklyn's first equestrian parade since the 1930s, with free pony rides for kids.

May 2020

Neighbors in Need

During the pandemic, stable horses pulled carts delivering food and supplies in Red Hook, with stops at Red Hook Farm, Red Hook Houses and the Joseph Miccio Community Center.

Heritage

A Park Built
for Horses

Prospect Park was designed with a bridle path, and Brooklyn once held horse parades hundreds strong. Helping Hooves carries that tradition forward.

  1. 1917

    Prospect Park Stable

    Prospect Park Stable is founded. It's the name the family would restore a century later.

  2. 1935

    A Parade Hundreds Strong

    A parade sponsored by the Brooklyn Daily Eagle and the ASPCA draws 252 horses, some 500 dogs, and a goat pulling a cart.

  3. 2018

    A Name Restored

    John Quadrozzi Jr. buys the former Kensington Stables and restores its 1917 name, Prospect Park Stable. His daughter Xiana, who took her first riding lessons there at age five, joins as program director.

  4. 2019

    Back in the Park

    The Equine Rally revives Brooklyn's horse parade, compost from the stable reaches Red Hook's gardens, and the GallopNYC therapeutic riding partnership begins.

  5. 2020

    Neighbors & New Riders

    Horse-drawn deliveries reach Red Hook neighbors during the pandemic, and young riders fill the summer immersion program.

  6. Today

    Helping Hooves

    Helping Hoof, Inc., a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, carries the mission forward alongside Be•Brooklyn Equine.
