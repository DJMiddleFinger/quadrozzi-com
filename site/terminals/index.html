Terminals

In 2013, Quadrozzi Enterprises represented seven multi-user waterfront terminals in Brooklyn and Queens: more than 30 acres of industrial land, with “an emphasis on sustainability & community.”

GBX

Gowanus Bay Terminal

In Red Hook, between the mouth of the Gowanus Canal and Erie Basin. Visit the GBX site.

BBX

Barbadoes Basin Terminal

On Jamaica Bay at Rockaway Beach. Once home to the famed Brooklyn Day Camp.

VBX

Vernam Basin Terminal

On Jamaica Bay, across from the Barbadoes Basin Reserve, with open bay, bridge and Manhattan views.

GCX

Gowanus Canal Terminal

On the Gowanus Canal, beneath the elevated Smith–9th Streets subway station. It appears in a scene from Goodfellas.

NCX

Newtown Creek Terminal

In Long Island City, along the LIRR tracks, with a large bulk silo for cement and fly ash.

CICX

Coney Island Creek Terminal

The first property you see entering Coney Island, marked by its tall coal silos.

Metropolitan Ave

Ridgewood, Queens

3.69 acres along Newtown Creek, ready for almost anything.

Land & sea

With decades of sea-freight experience, Quadrozzi representatives moved bulk cargo from Europe, South America and Mexico up the Eastern Seaboard into New York. On land, trucking skills passed down through generations moved concrete, sand and stone, rubble, rock salt, bulk powders and specialty equipment throughout the city.

After Sandy

After Hurricane Sandy in 2012, the company called for rebuilding smarter in flood zones: keep living space and mechanicals above the flood plain, build with concrete for strength and fire resistance, and replace wooden boardwalks with concrete ones that double as sea walls.
