Events

GBX offers a setting no ballroom can match. In 2002, Zacho Dance Theater staged an aerial performance here: dancers scaled the building while 100-foot video images of Red Hook were projected across its walls.

From private events to cultural programs, we’ll work with you on a plan that fits the site.

  • Private & corporate events
  • Brand activations & launches
  • Performances & installations
  • Community & cultural programs

All events are subject to site review, and to the permits and insurance that apply.
