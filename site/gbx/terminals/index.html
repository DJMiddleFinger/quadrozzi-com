Terminals

In 2013, Quadrozzi Enterprises represented seven multi-user waterfront terminals in Brooklyn and Queens: more than 30 acres of industrial land, with “an emphasis on sustainability & community.”

Land & sea

With decades of sea-freight experience, Quadrozzi representatives moved bulk cargo from Europe, South America and Mexico up the Eastern Seaboard into New York. On land, trucking skills passed down through generations moved concrete, sand and stone, rubble, rock salt, bulk powders and specialty equipment throughout the city.

After Sandy

After Hurricane Sandy in 2012, the company called for rebuilding smarter in flood zones: keep living space and mechanicals above the flood plain, build with concrete for strength and fire resistance, and replace wooden boardwalks with concrete ones that double as sea walls.
